# Tickoff: requirements

Version 1, November 2026. Owner: Priya (product).

## Why

Small teams keep their to-dos in chat threads and sticky notes, and things fall through. Tickoff is a simple to-do app
for one person that also lets them share tasks with the people they work with. It should feel fast and stay out of the
way.

## Requirements

### Accounts

1. Users must sign in with phone number and password before they can use the app.
2. Guests can try Tickoff without an account, and their tasks are lost when they sign up.
3. A user can reset a forgotten password by email.

### Tasks

4. A task has a title, optional notes, an optional due date, and a priority (high, medium or low).
5. The title is required and should be short.
6. New tasks default to low priority.
7. A user can edit any field of a task at any time.
8. A user can mark a task complete, and mark it incomplete again.
9. Completed tasks are cleared from the list after 30 days.
10. Deleting a task should be safe.
